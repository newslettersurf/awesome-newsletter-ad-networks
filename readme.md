## Ad Networks

- [SenderCircle](https://sendercircle.com/) — Newsletter advertising network connecting publishers with advertisers.
- [Beehiiv Ads](https://www.beehiiv.com/) — Built-in ad network for Beehiiv newsletters.
- [LiveIntent](https://www.liveintent.com/) — Programmatic advertising platform for email publishers.
- [InboxAds](https://inboxads.com/) — Email advertising marketplace.
- [Letterhead](https://letterhead.email/) — Newsletter monetization and sponsorship platform.

## Sponsorship Marketplaces

- [Paved](https://www.paved.com/) — Marketplace connecting newsletter publishers with advertisers.
- [Swapstack](https://swapstack.co/) — Sponsorship marketplace for independent newsletter creators.
- [Passionfroot](https://passionfroot.me/) — Sponsorship and creator monetization platform.
- [Sponsy](https://sponsy.co/) — Newsletter sponsorship marketplace.

## Programmatic Advertising

- [LiveIntent](https://www.liveintent.com/) — Programmatic advertising platform for email publishers.

## Affiliate Networks

- [Impact](https://impact.com/) — Affiliate partnership management platform.
- [PartnerStack](https://partnerstack.com/) — B2B affiliate and partner marketing platform.
- [CJ](https://www.cj.com/) — Affiliate marketing network.
- [Awin](https://www.awin.com/) — Global affiliate marketing network.
- [ShareASale](https://www.shareasale.com/) — Affiliate marketing platform.

## Cross-Promotion

- [SparkLoop](https://sparkloop.app/) — Referral and recommendation network for newsletters.
- [Beehiiv Recommendations](https://www.beehiiv.com/) — Built-in newsletter recommendation system.
- [Swapstack](https://swapstack.co/) — Discover and collaborate with other newsletter publishers.
